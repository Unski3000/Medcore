Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have a minimum order quantity?
No. We supply to facilities of all sizes — from solo practitioners ordering a single item to large hospital groups placing bulk orders. There is no minimum order requirement.
How long does delivery take?
In-stock items are dispatched within 48 hours. Local deliveries are typically 1–2 business days. Regional deliveries across Kenya take 2–4 business days. International orders vary by destination.
Are all products certified and approved?
Yes. All products in our catalogue meet ISO 13485, CE Mark, and relevant FDA standards. We provide full regulatory documentation including conformity declarations upon request.
Do you offer after-sales technical support?
Yes. Our biomedical engineering team provides installation, staff training, calibration and ongoing maintenance support for all major equipment purchases — both on-site and remotely.
Can I get a formal invoice or LPO?
Yes. We issue formal proforma invoices, LPOs and VAT receipts for all transactions. We are fully registered with KRA and can supply ETR receipts.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers (Co-operative Bank, Kimathi Branch, A/C: 01192846332200), M-Pesa Till No. 9255495, and cheques payable to MedCore Solutions. Flexible credit terms available for regular clients.
Do you offer discounts for bulk purchases?
Yes. We offer tiered volume pricing on all product lines. The more you order, the lower the unit price. Contact our sales team for a custom volume pricing proposal.
Can I become a distributor or reseller?
Yes, we welcome partnerships with distributors, resellers and healthcare procurement companies. Please contact us with your business details and we will share our partnership programme information.
